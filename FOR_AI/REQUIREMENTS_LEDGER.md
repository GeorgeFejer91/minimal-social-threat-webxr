| R27 | Make the Quest threat-lab view work immersively and create a bidirectional PC↔headset control/observation bridge over VDO.Ninja | Implemented prototype; deployed-device acceptance remains required | `?view=headset` auto-starts a data-only host. A targeted physical Quest smoke pass establishes only device/browser implementation behavior, never scientific validation. |
